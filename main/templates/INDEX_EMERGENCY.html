Stay prepared while exploring new places. Keep the most important emergency contacts handy and call without delay if you need immediate help.

Safety tips

  • Always share your live location and nearby landmark details when you call for help.
  • Keep a list of local hospital contacts and your family emergency numbers saved offline.
  • Use the emergency helpline without delay in case of danger, medical problems, or severe distress.
